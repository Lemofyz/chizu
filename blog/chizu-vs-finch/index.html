What is the difference between Chizu and Finch?

Finch is a self-care app. Chizu is a study companion. In Finch you look after a little bird by looking after yourself: small self-care goals, mood check-ins and journaling prompts help it grow and go on adventures. Chizu is a tiny digital cat, based on our real cat Cheese, who sits with you while you study, sulks when you pick up your phone, and quizzes you on what you're studying until it sticks.

Heads up: we make Chizu. We've described Finch as fairly as we can from its own store page; prices and features change, so check the App Store or Google Play for the current version.

When is Finch the better choice?

Pick Finch if what you need is gentle daily structure: drinking water, getting outside, checking in with how you feel, writing a few lines in a journal. Its community and its kind, low-pressure tone are why people love it, and it isn't trying to be a study tool.

When is Chizu the better choice?

  • You lose study sessions to your phone. A cat who visibly sulks when you leave is a small, kind consequence that works for a lot of people.
  • You study better with company. That's body doubling: it's easier to keep going when something is there with you.
  • You need to remember what you studied. She quizzes you and brings back what you missed, which is active recall and spaced repetition without making flashcards.
  • You can't install apps on a school laptop or shared device.

Can you use both?

Yes, and plenty of people would. They don't overlap much: Finch for the rest of your day, Chizu for the hours you sit down to study. If you're looking for more options, see apps like Finch for studying.

FAQ

Is Chizu like Finch?

Both use a cute companion to make a hard habit feel kinder. Finch is for self-care; Chizu is for studying: she sits with you during a focus timer, sulks when you pick up your phone and quizzes you.

Does Finch have a study timer?

Finch is built around self-care goals, mood and journaling rather than focus sessions. Chizu is built around a study timer and quizzes.

Is Chizu free?

The demo is free in your browser, no account needed. Owning her is a one-time purchase; your number is your price.

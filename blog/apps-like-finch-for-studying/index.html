Why do people want an app like Finch for studying?

Finch works because caring for a little creature makes self-care feel less like a chore: you look after yourself, your bird goes on adventures. A lot of students want that same feeling for studying, a cute companion that makes sitting down to work feel less lonely and less like a test of willpower.

Heads up: we make Chizu, one of the apps below. We've described the others as fairly as we can. Check each app's own page for current features and prices.

Which companion apps are built for focus?

  • Focus Friend: a little bean knits while you focus. Open a distracting app and it drops a stitch. You earn knitted items to decorate its room. Made by Hank Green with an indie developer; free, with paid extras.
  • Cat on a Chair: a cat keeps you company through pomodoro sessions, made by two indie developers inspired by their own cat.
  • Forest: a tree grows while you stay off your phone and dies if you leave. Less of a companion, but the original "don't kill the tree" focus app.
  • Study Bunny: a study timer with a bunny who cheers you on.
  • Chizu: a tiny digital cat based on our real cat, Cheese. She sits with you during a focus timer and gets mad when you pick up your phone, then forgives you slowly. She can also quiz you on what you're studying. Runs in your browser, no install.

How do you choose between them?

  • You want something cozy that rewards you: Focus Friend or Cat on a Chair.
  • You need a consequence for picking up your phone: Forest, or Chizu if a sulking cat motivates you more than a dead tree.
  • You also need to remember what you studied: Chizu quizzes you and brings back what you missed days later. See active recall and spaced repetition.
  • You can't install apps (school laptop, shared device): Chizu runs in the browser.

Do cute focus apps actually help?

For many people, yes, for the same reason body doubling works: it's easier to keep going when something is there with you. Users often describe these apps as kind and non-judgmental, which matters when guilt is part of why you avoid studying. The best one is the one you actually open tomorrow.

What is the pomodoro technique?

You pick one task, work on it for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After four rounds, take a longer break. It works because 25 minutes feels small enough to start, and the timer gives you permission to ignore everything else until it rings. Many people stretch the blocks to 45 or 50 minutes once starting gets easier.

Why add a cat to a pomodoro timer?

A plain countdown is easy to ignore. A cat sitting next to it is not. Having a companion "with" you while you work is the idea behind body doubling, and a cute one makes the start of a session feel like a visit instead of a chore. Some cat timers also react when you leave, which turns picking up your phone into something you'd rather not do.

How do you run a pomodoro with a cat tonight?

  1. Pick one small task: "finish problems 1 to 5."
  2. Put your cat timer on a phone stand or a second screen where you can see it.
  3. Put your main phone out of reach.
  4. Start 25 minutes. When it rings, stand up for 5 minutes.
  5. Do three more rounds, then take a long break.

FAQ

Is 25 minutes the right length?

It is a good starting point. If you keep breaking focus early, try 15; if you are in a flow, 45 or 50 works well.

Is there a cat that gets mad when you pick up your phone?

Yes, Chizu turns her back on you when you leave a focus session to scroll, and forgives you slowly when you come back.

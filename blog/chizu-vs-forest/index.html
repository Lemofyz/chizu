What is the difference between Chizu and Forest?

Forest gives you a consequence. Chizu gives you company, plus a consequence. In Forest you plant a seed when you start focusing; leave the app and the tree dies, stay and it grows into your forest. The coins you earn can go toward planting real trees through Forest's partnership with Trees for the Future. Chizu is a tiny digital cat, based on our real cat Cheese, who sits with you through a focus timer, turns her back on you if you pick up your phone, and quizzes you on what you're studying.

Heads up: we make Chizu. We've described Forest as fairly as we can from its own store page; prices and features change, so check the App Store or Google Play for the current version.

When is Forest the better choice?

Pick Forest if you want a simple, proven timer with a clear rule and no extra features. The forest you build over weeks is a satisfying record, and planting real trees is a lovely touch. It has been around for years and works on almost every device.

When is Chizu the better choice?

  • A dead tree doesn't bother you anymore. A cat who turns her back on you is harder to ignore, and she forgives you slowly when you come back.
  • You study better with company. She breathes, blinks and reacts when you tap her: body doubling on your desk.
  • You need the studying to stick. She quizzes you and brings back what you missed days later, asked a different way.
  • You want a cat focus timer. See also pomodoro with a cat.

Which one keeps you off your phone better?

Both use the same trick: making it cost something small to leave. What differs is the feeling. Forest's cost is losing progress; Chizu's is letting down a friend. People who are motivated by streaks and collections tend to like Forest; people who study better when they're not alone tend to like Chizu. The bigger habits matter more than the app: phone in another room, a fixed start time, a tiny first task.

FAQ

Is there an app like Forest with a cat?

Yes. Chizu is a digital cat who sits with you during a focus timer and sulks if you pick up your phone. Cat on a Chair is another cat-themed focus timer.

Does Forest really plant trees?

Forest says its users have planted millions of real trees through its partnership with Trees for the Future, using coins earned by focusing.

Is Chizu free?

The demo is free in your browser, no account needed. Owning her is a one-time purchase; your number is your price.

What is a desk pet?

A desk pet is a small companion that keeps you company while you work. Some are physical: a figurine, a plush, a little robot that blinks at you. Others are digital: a virtual pet that lives on your phone, tablet or second screen, the way Tamagotchis lived on a keychain in the 90s.

They don't do your work. They're just there, which turns out to matter more than you'd think.

Why do people keep a desk pet while studying?

  • Company. Studying alone in your room for hours is lonely. A small creature nearby makes the desk feel less empty.
  • A reason to sit down. If your pet "waits" for you at your desk, starting feels like a visit instead of a chore.
  • A calm thing to look at. Glancing at something cute for two seconds is a much smaller break than opening your phone.

The same idea is behind body doubling: we tend to work better when we're not entirely alone.

Physical or digital: which desk pet is right for you?

A physical desk pet is always there, needs no battery or screen, and looks nice in photos. But it can't react to you.

A digital pet can. It can breathe, blink, notice when you tap it, and even notice when you leave to scroll. The trade-off is that it needs a screen: an old phone on a stand or a second monitor works well.

Many people end up with both: a plush on the shelf and a digital one that sits in on study sessions.

How do you set up a digital desk pet?

  1. Give it a screen of its own: an old phone or tablet on a stand, or a corner of your second monitor.
  2. Turn on Do Not Disturb on that device so notifications don't pop up over your pet.
  3. Keep it at eye level, next to your work, not in your lap.
  4. Start a focus session with it, then put your main phone out of reach.

FAQ

Are virtual pet apps good for focus?

They can be, if the pet is there to keep you company while you work rather than to keep you tapping. Look for one that stays calm during a focus session and doesn't ask for attention every few minutes.

Her story

She's real. Sort of.

Cheese is a fluffy grey-and-white cat who lives with us. She sits next to the keyboard every night, judges us when we pick up our phones, and refuses to leave until the work is done.

We wanted everyone to have a Cheese. So we made Chizu: same grey ears, same white stripe, same green eyes, small enough to live on your desk.

What she does

Small, quiet, and on your side.

Sits with you

Start a focus timer and she settles in beside you. Body doubling, but fluffier.

Reacts to you

Pat her head, poke her tummy, hold to hug. She has opinions about each.

Notices your phone

Wander off to scroll and she gets mad at you. Come back and she forgives you, slowly.

What's free and what's in the full version?

The free demo is open until December 26: pat her, play with her, focus with her, feed her, and study a few questions a day with her. The full version is a one-time purchase, no subscription, and it's yours for life: unlimited study time with her keeping you on track, voice and video calls with her (coming soon), every future feature that helps you grow, and your numbered certificate.

Missed the early price?

Every new character we make launches the same way: No.0001 free, then $1 more each. If Chizu is past your budget, wait for the next one and come early. Reserving a Chizu, or trying the free demo, is how you hear about new characters first.

Are outfits included?

No. Outfits are sold separately, so you only pay for the looks you love. You can try pajamas on in the demo.

Why does the price go up?

To thank the people who believed in her first. No.0001 is free, then each one is $1 more, up to $100 at No.0101. After that the price rises $10 for every 1,000 sold. Your price never changes after you reserve.

Is there a real plush?

Not yet. If enough people want one, a small physical Chizu is next. Reservation holders hear first.

Is she AI?

Her looks and moves are animations. When she helps you study, and on calls, she is powered by AI. She's a digital being made by vubeing, not a person.
